NuviPal Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 23, 2026

Last updated: August 23, 2026

NuviPal is an iOS app for logging meals. You photograph or describe a meal, an AI model estimates its ingredients and nutrition, and NuviPal keeps the result in a food diary with a daily score and progress charts. This Privacy Policy explains how NuviPal handles information.

Meal Photos and AI Analysis

When you ask NuviPal to analyze a meal, the photo you took (or the note you typed, or both) is sent to NuviPal's analysis gateway, which runs on Google Cloud, and from there to OpenRouter, which forwards it to the AI model that produces the estimate. This is the only time your meal data leaves your device to a service that is not Apple's.

NuviPal does not store your photos or notes on its gateway. They are held in memory only for the length of the request and are not written to any database, log, or file. Requests are routed only to model providers that are configured not to retain prompts.

Analysis is not automatic. Nothing is sent until you tap to analyze a meal.

Your Account

Analysis requires signing in with Apple. NuviPal does not ask Apple for your name or your email address and does not receive them. What NuviPal receives is the anonymous account identifier Apple issues for this app, which cannot be used to identify you in any other app or service.

NuviPal also stores a one-way cryptographic hash of that identifier so the free trial scans cannot be reset by deleting and reinstalling the app. The hash cannot be reversed back to your Apple account.

Authentication is provided by Firebase Authentication, a Google service.

What NuviPal's Server Stores

NuviPal's gateway keeps a small record for your account so that analysis can work: how many analyses you have run, how much AI usage they cost against your allowance, how many free scans remain, whether your subscription is active and when it expires, and an identifier that links your purchase to your account.

NuviPal's server does not store your meals, your photos, your notes, your nutrition history, your weight, or any health data. Those never reach it in a stored form.

Your Diary

Your meal history, meal photos, nutrition totals, day scores, weight entries, and app settings are stored on your device. When you are signed in to iCloud, they also sync through Apple's CloudKit service in your private iCloud database, so the same diary appears on your other devices.

iCloud sync is not a backup. A meal deleted on one device is deleted everywhere, and deleted meals cannot be restored. NuviPal also removes the full-resolution image of older meals automatically to limit how much space the diary uses; the meal and its nutrition data remain.

NuviPal does not operate its own servers for storing your diary.

Apple Health

If you allow it, NuviPal writes the nutrition of the meals you log into Apple Health, and reads your body weight and your active and resting energy from Apple Health to show them alongside your intake.

Health data stays between your device and Apple Health. NuviPal never sends health data to its gateway, to OpenRouter, to any AI model, or to any other third party, and never uses it for advertising or marketing. You can withdraw access at any time in the Health app under Sharing.

Subscriptions and Purchases

NuviPal offers auto-renewable subscriptions through Apple In-App Purchase. Payments, renewals, cancellations, refunds, and subscription management are handled by Apple.

To unlock analysis, the app sends the purchase receipt Apple signed to NuviPal's gateway, which verifies Apple's signature and records whether the subscription is active and when it expires. Apple also notifies the gateway when a subscription renews, lapses, or is refunded. NuviPal never receives your payment details.

NuviPal does not use a third-party subscription analytics or entitlement service.

Analytics and Tracking

NuviPal does not include advertising SDKs, third-party analytics SDKs, or ad tracking, and does not track you across apps or websites. NuviPal does not sell personal data.

Apple and Third-Party Services

NuviPal relies on Apple services such as iCloud, CloudKit, StoreKit, Sign in with Apple, and HealthKit; on Google's Firebase for authentication and the analysis gateway; and on OpenRouter for access to AI models. Your use of those services is also subject to their own privacy policies.

External Links

NuviPal may include links to external pages such as privacy, terms, support, or Apple subscription management resources. If you open an external link, your use of that website or service is subject to its own privacy policy and terms.

Children's Privacy

NuviPal is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us.

Data Deletion

You can delete individual meals from within the app, and you can export your whole diary to a CSV file at any time.

Deleting your account in the app removes the record NuviPal's gateway keeps for you, revokes NuviPal's access to your Apple account, and deletes your authentication record. Your diary is separate: to remove it, delete your meals in the app, or remove the app's data from your device and from iCloud using the iOS and iCloud settings.

Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription. Subscriptions are cancelled in your Apple account settings.
