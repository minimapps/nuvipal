NuviPal Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 23, 2026

Last updated: September 23, 2026

NuviPal is an iOS app for logging meals. You photograph or describe a meal, an AI model estimates its ingredients and nutrition, and NuviPal keeps the result in a food diary with a daily score and progress charts. This Privacy Policy explains how NuviPal handles information.

Meal Photos and AI Analysis

With your permission, NuviPal sends the meal photos, descriptions, and corrections you submit to its cloud service and third-party AI providers to estimate nutrition. Corrections may also include the meal's existing analysis. This does not upload your entire diary.

NuviPal does not store your photos or notes on its gateway. They are held in memory only for the length of the request and are not deliberately written to a database, log, or file by the analysis service. Third-party providers handle the content under their own privacy policies.

NuviPal asks for AI data-sharing permission before sending your first analysis request. After you allow it, Instant Analysis can start processing when you add a meal. You can turn off Instant Analysis or withdraw AI-sharing permission in Settings → AI Analysis. Withdrawal prevents future requests; it cannot recall content already sent. Your saved diary remains available without AI-sharing permission.

Your Account

Analysis requires signing in with Apple. NuviPal does not ask Apple for your name or your email address and does not request them for your profile. NuviPal uses the account identifier Apple provides to manage your sign-in, analysis allowance, and subscription.

NuviPal retains a pseudonymous trial-usage record to prevent repeated free trials, including after account deletion. This record does not contain your meals or photos.

What NuviPal's Server Stores

NuviPal's gateway keeps a small record for your account so that analysis can work: how many analyses you have run, how much AI usage they cost against your allowance, how many free scans remain, whether your subscription is active and when it expires, and an identifier that links your purchase to your account.

NuviPal's server does not store your meals, your photos, your notes, your nutrition history, your weight, or any health data. Those never reach it in a stored form.

Your Diary

Your meal history, meal photos, nutrition totals, day scores, weight entries, and app settings are stored on your device. Meal records, photos, and manually entered weights can also sync through Apple's CloudKit service in your private iCloud database. Device preferences are not all synced between devices.

iCloud sync is not a backup. A meal deleted on one device is deleted everywhere, and deleted meals cannot be restored. NuviPal also removes the full-resolution image of older meals automatically to limit how much space the diary uses; the meal and its nutrition data remain.

NuviPal does not operate its own servers for storing your diary.

Apple Health

If you allow it, NuviPal writes the nutrition of the meals you log into Apple Health, and reads your body weight and your active and resting energy from Apple Health to show them alongside your intake.

Data read from Apple Health stays between your device and Apple Health. NuviPal never sends those readings to its gateway or other third parties, and never uses them for advertising or marketing. You can withdraw access at any time in the Health app under Sharing.

Subscriptions and Purchases

NuviPal offers auto-renewable subscriptions through Apple In-App Purchase. Payments, renewals, cancellations, refunds, and subscription management are handled by Apple.

To unlock analysis, the app sends the purchase receipt Apple signed to NuviPal's gateway, which verifies Apple's signature and records whether the subscription is active and when it expires. Apple also notifies the gateway when a subscription renews, lapses, or is refunded. NuviPal never receives your payment details.

NuviPal does not use a third-party subscription analytics or entitlement service.

Analytics and Tracking

NuviPal does not include advertising SDKs, third-party analytics SDKs, or ad tracking, and does not track you across apps or websites. NuviPal does not sell personal data.

Apple and Third-Party Services

NuviPal uses Apple services for iCloud sync, sign-in, purchases, and Health; Google's Firebase for authentication and cloud request processing; and OpenRouter and its AI providers for meal analysis. These services handle the information described above under their own privacy policies. The AI model provider may change over time.

External Links

NuviPal may include links to external pages such as privacy, terms, support, or Apple subscription management resources. If you open an external link, your use of that website or service is subject to its own privacy policy and terms.

Children's Privacy

NuviPal is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us.

Data Deletion

You can delete individual meals from within the app, and you can export your whole diary to a CSV file at any time.

Deleting your account in the app removes the record NuviPal's gateway keeps for you, revokes NuviPal's access to your Apple account, and deletes your authentication record. The pseudonymous trial-usage record described above is retained to prevent repeated free trials. Your diary is separate: to remove it, delete your meals in the app, or remove the app's data from your device and from iCloud using the iOS and iCloud settings.

Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription. Subscriptions are cancelled in your Apple account settings.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated last-updated date.
