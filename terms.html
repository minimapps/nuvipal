Estimates Are Estimates

Nutrition figures in NuviPal are produced by AI models from a photo or a written description. They are approximations, not measurements. The app cannot see inside a dish, cannot weigh it, and cannot know the recipe, so ingredients, portion weights, calories, macronutrients, salt, sugar, fibre, and the resulting scores may be inaccurate, sometimes substantially.

NuviPal cannot identify allergens and must never be relied on for allergy safety. It also cannot reliably detect whether something is safe to eat.

You are responsible for reviewing and correcting what the app estimates. Meals and analysis results may be saved automatically; you can review and edit their ingredients afterward.

Meal Scores

NuviPal grades meals and days using a scoring model derived from publicly available nutrition scoring approaches. Scores are a rough guide to the balance of what you ate. They are not an assessment of whether a food is good or bad for you personally, and they are not endorsed by, affiliated with, or certified by any health authority or standards body.
