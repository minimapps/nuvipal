NuviPal

Photograph a meal. Get its nutrition.

NuviPal is a food diary that does the tedious part for you. Take a photo of what you are about to eat and an AI model estimates the ingredients, the portion weight, and the nutrition – no searching a database, no weighing, no picking from a list.

Features

  • Analyze from a photo. One shot, and the meal comes back broken down into ingredients with calories, protein, fat, carbs, sugar, fibre, and salt.
  • Or just describe it. No photo? Type what you ate. You can also add a note to a photo to say what the camera cannot see.
  • Correct anything. Tell NuviPal what it got wrong in plain language and it re-estimates, or edit any ingredient by hand.
  • A score for the day. Every meal is graded, and the day gets one score that reflects what you actually ate against what you still need.
  • Progress you can see. Charts for intake, weight, and the energy Apple Health records.
  • Apple Health. Meals you log are written straight into Health, so the rest of your apps see them too.
  • Private iCloud sync. Your diary follows you to your other devices through your own iCloud account.
  • Your data is yours. Export the whole diary to CSV whenever you like.

NuviPal is free to download. Your diary, the day score, progress charts, and Apple Health are free, and new accounts get free analyses to try. NuviPal Pro, an optional subscription, is what buys the AI analysis after that.

Support

How do I log a meal?

Tap the camera button, photograph your food, and tap Analyze. You can review and edit every ingredient before saving it to the diary.

The estimate is wrong. What do I do?

Type a correction – "this is a large portion", "there is no oil in it", "the bread is rye" – and NuviPal re-estimates. You can also tap any ingredient and edit its weight or nutrition directly.

Why do I need to sign in?

Analysis runs on AI models that cost money per request, so it is tied to an account. NuviPal uses Sign in with Apple and does not ask for your name or email address. Everything else in the app works without an account.

What does the subscription include?

NuviPal Pro unlocks AI meal analysis, with a weekly allowance of analyses. The diary, the day score, progress charts, Apple Health, and CSV export are free and stay free.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the NuviPal Pro screen and tap Restore.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. Open the iOS Settings app → your name → Subscriptions, or see Apple's guide to managing subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

How does sync work?

When you are signed in to iCloud, your meals, photos, and weight entries sync automatically across your devices through your private iCloud account. Note that this is sync, not backup: a meal you delete on one device is deleted on all of them, and it cannot be restored.

Where are my photos and meals stored?

On your device and in your own iCloud. NuviPal's server never stores them – a photo passes through only for the seconds it takes to analyze it. See the Privacy Policy for the detail.

How do I export or delete my data?

Open Profile → Your Data and tap Export Meals to save the whole diary as a CSV file. To delete your account, open Profile → Account and tap Delete Account.
